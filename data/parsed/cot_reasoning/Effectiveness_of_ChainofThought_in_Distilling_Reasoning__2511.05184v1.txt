 The output probabilities produced at the output 
 layers of these models are used to compute the distillation loss. The error derivative of the distillation 
 loss is then back-propagated through the student 
 LLM to update its weights and minimise the distillation loss. The key difference between KD+CoT 
 and vanilla white-box KD is that the former incorporates rationales in the training data, while the 
 latter does not.

 the training data for KD is expected to elicit the 
 teacher LLM s reasoning capability during training, 
 effectively distilling it to the student LLM by minimising the distillation loss between the models 
 output probabilities.
